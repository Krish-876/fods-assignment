--- Deliverable 7: Discussion ---
  Monotonicity check for lam >= lam* = 1.0000e-01:
    train RMSE strictly non-decreasing: True -> phrase: 'monotonically increase'
    test  RMSE strictly non-decreasing: True  -> phrase: 'monotonically increase'

  Discussion (P3): L1 regularisation (Lasso) produces exact zeros because the subdifferential optimality condition for w_j=0 requires |Phi_j^T r| <= lam, where the diamond-shaped L1 ball forces solutions to touch coordinate axes, whereas the smooth L2 ball in Ridge only shrinks weights without setting them to zero. The coefficient path (D3) demonstrates this progressive pruning: the number of ~zero coefficients (|w_j| < 2*eta*lam) grows from 0 at small lam to 4 at lam=0.1, reaching 8 at the largest lam=1.0e+00. In the D5 comparison on the Legendre basis, Lasso achieves a test RMSE of 0.098544 with 4 coefficients having |w_j| < 0.01, whereas Ridge yields a test RMSE of 0.142233 with only 1 coefficient below 0.01. Beyond lam*=1.00e-01, excessive regularisation removes true signal, so train RMSE and test RMSE both monotonically increase as the model underfits.
