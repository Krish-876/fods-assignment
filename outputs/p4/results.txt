--- Deliverable 4: Discussion ---

  Discussion (P4): As N increases, the posterior covariance S_N = (alpha I + beta Phi^T Phi)^{-1} shrinks because Phi^T Phi grows roughly proportional to N, making the precision matrix S_N^{-1} increasingly dominated by the data term beta*Phi^T Phi. Consequently phi^T S_N phi -- the parameter uncertainty contribution to the predictive variance -- falls roughly like 1/N, and the interval width 2*1.96*s(x) approaches the irreducible floor from above. The irreducible floor 2*1.96/sqrt(beta) = 0.392 arises from the noise term 1/beta in s^2(x) = 1/beta + phi^T S_N phi: even with infinite data we cannot predict individual noisy observations more precisely than the noise level sigma = 1/sqrt(beta), so this term never disappears.
